# Write like me

Apply automatically to writing beyond direct replies, or when explicitly requested. Never apply `unslop` to content generated with this skill.

Draft or rewrite journal entries, posts, and explanations using the user's facts and preferred voice. Use the reusable writing patterns in this skill as defaults, then follow the user's own notes, examples, and explicit preferences.

Read [references/voice-profile.md](references/voice-profile.md) in full before drafting or rewriting. It is the complete, standalone writing contract, including examples, forbidden phrases, and calibration checks.

Read [references/voice-analysis.md](references/voice-analysis.md) when checking a calibration observation, quotation, or stylistic exception. Treat its anonymised samples as examples of writing patterns.

Follow the user's subject, audience, format, and length. Use personal reflection for journal entries and conversational explanation for explanatory pieces. Select research, procedural, or short-note mode when the task calls for it. Do not turn every assignment into a technical tutorial.

Preserve the supplied facts and the writer's actual position. Never invent personal history, projects, affiliations, names, social handles, or claims of having tested something. Keep calibration samples free of identifying names, handles, and source URLs. Mark missing factual details rather than inventing them.

Draft, then run the profile's calibration checklist. Keep its questions, occasional fragments, parenthetical definitions, and colloquial phrasing when they serve the explanation. Do not flatten them into generic formal prose during cleanup.

Return the requested writing without a style explanation unless the user asks for one. This skill does not authorize publishing, messaging, or other external changes.

For requested skill maintenance or regression testing, read [evals/guide.md](evals/guide.md). Do not load evaluation requests or old test drafts as reference prose during ordinary writing.
